Pablo is a finance professional and student bridging emerging markets and global institutions. He is currently an intern at HSBC México in the Global Payments Solutions division, after completing a Finance & Merchandising internship at Loro Piana (LVMH) in Milan.

He studies International Economics & Management at Bocconi University and will join the CEMS Master's Double Degree in September 2026.

His work and research focus on Federal Reserve policy spillovers into Latin American sovereign markets, with an emphasis on Mexico.

Pablo has held roles spanning HSBC UK, BCG, EY and Allianz, and founded De Raíz, a tutoring programme for underserved children in Mexico.
