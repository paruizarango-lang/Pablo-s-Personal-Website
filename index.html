About

Finance & sustainability-driven young global leader

Pablo Ruiz Arango is a finance and sustainability-driven young global leader, recognized for his cross-border academic excellence and professional impact across Europe, North America, and Latin America. He is currently a Summer Analyst at HSBC UK in Premier Banking within the Sustainability Office, where he was named Intern of the Year 2025 for advancing the firm's Net-Zero strategy and organizing high-profile initiatives, including a flagship panel with Tim Lord, Head of Climate Change. In October, he will represent Spain at the BCG Global DEI Program, joining a 60-person global cohort.

Beyond banking, Pablo is a Global Shaper at the World Economic Forum, where he has moderated international forums on AI and creative industries and partnered with Milan Municipality to design youth engagement initiatives reaching more than 1,400 students. He has also held leadership roles as Partnership and KRM Director at 180 Degrees Consulting and as Head of FICC and Equities at Bocconi's Asset Management Club.

Having grown up in Mexico, studied in Italy, advised clients in China, and soon moving to Canada, Pablo thrives in multicultural environments and is fluent in Spanish, English, Italian, and French. His academic path includes a BSc in Economics at Bocconi University, a spring program at HEC Paris, an exchange at the University of Toronto, and the CFI Corporate Finance Certificate.

A passionate advocate for sustainability and financial inclusion, Pablo is an active speaker and panelist, a member of the Nova Talent Under 35 network, and will contribute to global press and event operations at the Milano Winter Olympics 2026.
